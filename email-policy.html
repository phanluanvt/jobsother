Email & Job Alerts Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

JobsOther offers optional alerts to help users discover newly available jobs.

1. Creating an alert

When you submit an email address for a job alert, you request messages related to the keyword, location or job preferences associated with that alert. An account is not necessarily required.

2. What we may send

Alerts may contain new or relevant job listings, links back to JobsOther and service information needed to manage the alert. External application links may lead to third-party websites.

3. Frequency and availability

Frequency can vary based on available matching jobs and service operation. We do not guarantee that every matching job will be included or that alerts will arrive at a particular time.

4. Unsubscribing

You can unsubscribe using the unsubscribe option provided in an alert where available, or contact support@jobsother.com. We may retain minimal suppression information needed to respect an unsubscribe request.

5. Browser push notifications

Browser notifications are separate from email. They require browser permission. You can revoke that permission at any time in your browser or device notification settings.

6. Third-party services

We may use service providers to deliver email or browser notifications. Their processing is limited to providing those services subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices.

7. Contact

For alert or unsubscribe questions, contact support@jobsother.com.
