Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how JobsOther handles information when you use our job-search website, location tools, email alerts and browser notifications.

1. Information we collect

We may receive information you provide directly, such as job-search keywords, city or postal code, email address and alert preferences. We may also receive basic technical information such as browser type, device information, referring pages and site interactions.

2. How we use information

We use information to operate and improve job search, personalize relevant results, provide requested alerts, maintain security, measure site performance and understand how visitors use JobsOther.

3. Location information

You may enter a location manually or choose “Use my location.” Precise browser location is requested only after your browser asks for permission. You can deny or revoke location permission through your browser settings.

4. Email and browser job alerts

If you create an alert, we use the contact or notification information necessary to deliver it. You may unsubscribe from email alerts or disable browser notifications at any time. See our Email & Alerts Policy.

5. Job listings and third-party services

JobsOther aggregates job information from third-party providers. When you follow an Apply or View Job link, you may leave JobsOther. The destination site controls its own privacy practices. We may also use service providers for analytics, job data, hosting, translation and notifications.

6. Cookies and analytics

JobsOther may use cookies, local storage and similar technologies for site functionality, preferences, analytics and alert features. We use Google Analytics to understand aggregate website usage. See our Cookie Policy.

7. Data retention and security

We retain information only as reasonably necessary for the purposes described here, legal obligations, security and dispute resolution. We use reasonable safeguards, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices

You can choose not to provide browser location, disable cookies through browser controls, unsubscribe from alerts and disable push notifications. Where applicable, you may contact us regarding access, correction or deletion requests.

9. Children

JobsOther is a general employment-search service and is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service changes. The “Last updated” date above indicates the latest revision.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to support@jobsother.com.

Important: JobsOther is a job-search service, not the employer for third-party listings. Information submitted on an employer or external application website is governed by that website.
