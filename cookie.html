Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

This policy explains how JobsOther uses cookies, local storage and similar browser technologies.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of data a website can store through your browser. Similar technologies, including local storage, can remember preferences or support website functionality.

2. How JobsOther uses them

We may use these technologies to keep the website functioning, remember choices, prevent repetitive prompts, understand site usage and support job-alert features.

3. Analytics

JobsOther uses Google Analytics to measure website traffic and interactions. Analytics technologies can process information about pages viewed, approximate location, device/browser information and referring sources.

4. Alerts and preferences

Local storage may remember whether you dismissed a notification prompt. Browser push notifications require separate browser permission and can be disabled through your browser or operating-system settings.

5. Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies and site data through browser settings. Blocking some storage may affect website features or saved preferences.

6. Updates

We may update this policy as technologies or service providers change.

7. Contact

Questions can be sent to support@jobsother.com.
